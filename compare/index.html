What you get with QuickCast

  • A free plan you can actually use. 15 recordings every month, downloads included, no watermark.
  • Share in seconds. Stop recording and your link is ready — QuickCast can copy it for you automatically.
  • Comments and reactions on every plan. Viewers can react and leave timestamped comments, even on Free.
  • One price, not per seat. Pro is $6/month billed yearly (₹720 in India) for unlimited recordings.
  • 4K when you need it. Studio adds 4K, 2-hour recordings and 500 GB for $11/month billed yearly.
  • Pay the way you pay. UPI, cards and net banking in India; PayPal or a card everywhere else.
  • Private by default. Your recordings are never used for AI training, and you can delete everything in one click.
