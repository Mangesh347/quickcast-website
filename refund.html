Refund Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Short version: if Pro isn't right for you, email us within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund. You can cancel any time to stop future charges.

First payment

New Pro subscriptions (monthly or yearly) can be refunded in full within 7 days of the first payment. Email team@quickcast.app from your QuickCast account's email address.

Renewals

Renewals aren't refundable once the new period has started, except where the law requires it — but you can cancel at any time and you won't be charged again. If you forgot to cancel a yearly renewal, write to us within 7 days of it and we'll help.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay or PayPal, usually within 5–10 business days. After a refund, your account moves to the Free plan; your recordings stay.

Problems with the service

If something we're responsible for stops QuickCast working for you, tell us — we'll fix it or refund you fairly.
