Questions

What happens when I reach the Free limits?

Free includes 15 recordings a month and 1 GB of storage. The monthly count resets on the 1st. Deleting a video frees storage, but not the month's recordings. When you hit a limit, you can wait for the reset or upgrade to Pro.

Do my videos expire?

Pro videos never expire. On Free, videos stay as long as you use QuickCast. If a free account is unused for 90 days, recordings that nobody watched in that time are deleted — we email you twice before that happens, and any activity resets the timer.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from Dashboard → Settings → Plan & Billing. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then move to Free. Your videos stay; if you're over the Free storage you won't be able to record new ones until you delete some.

How do I pay?

In India you pay in rupees with UPI, cards or net banking through Razorpay (prices include 18% GST). Everywhere else you pay in US dollars through PayPal — with a PayPal account or a card. We never see or store your card details.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — see our refund policy. If QuickCast doesn't work for you, write to us within 7 days of your first payment.
